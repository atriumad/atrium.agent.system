# Six ways to *talk to the team*

The same agents serve two audiences: the restaurant owner, in the Platform and the Mobile app, and the Atrium team, in the Manager. See [Product](../product).

| Way | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask** | Write to an agent with @. It answers with the data, its method and a recommendation | "@Sommelier how many new guests came back in September?" |
| **Approve** | Everything that goes out in the client's voice, or spends the client's money, lands in an inbox: approve, request changes or discard | Echo · reply to a 2★ review |
| **Receive alerts** | Agents warn when something crosses a threshold, with the cause and who is handling it | Host · the rating dropped from 4.6 to 4.5 in 7 days |
| **Read the brief** | Maître opens every week with the state of the five engines and three actions | Monday 8:00 |
| **Assign** | A result is requested; Maître distributes it among subagents | "I want to fill Tuesdays in November" |
| **Follow the log** | Every action, handoff and approval is recorded | Echo published the review reply at 09:41 |

## Where each one lives

| Way | Platform (owner) | Mobile (owner) | Manager (Atrium) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ask | Ask your strategist | Requests | Per client and portfolio |
| Approve | Management → approval queue | "Waiting on your OK" | Approvals |
| Receive alerts | Dashboard wins and flags | Push notifications | Needs attention |
| Read the brief | Reporting | Home → What's going on | Portfolio health |
| Assign | Optional, per client | Requests | Tasks and COG |
| Log and autonomy | Read the log | — | Read and adjust autonomy |

## Who can do what

| Way | Restaurant owner | Atrium team |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask | About their business | About the whole portfolio |
| Approve | Content and public replies | Everything, including ad spend |
| Receive alerts | About their business | About their assigned clients |
| Read the brief | Weekly and monthly report | Per client and portfolio |
| Assign | Optional, per client | Yes |
| Log and autonomy | Read the log | Read and adjust autonomy |
