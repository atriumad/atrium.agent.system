## The three tiers

| Tier | For | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 Discover** | Restaurants that are invisible, inconsistent or starting fresh. Builds presence and audience | Solve invisibility: earn a place on the mental shortlist before hunger strikes |
| **2 Retain** | Restaurants with traffic but no system to bring people back. Builds the owned relationship | Turn a one-time $28 ticket into an $840 relationship |
| **3 Growth** | Restaurants ready to turn marketing into measurable revenue. Every dollar tracked to orders | Point the audience from Discover and the list from Retain at specific revenue goals |

Monthly Reports and the 90-Day Roadmap are proposed to ship in every tier: every client needs to know what their investment produced.
