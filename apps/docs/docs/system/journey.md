# The journey as *measurable signals*

Each discovery moment is won with a concrete condition. If the condition can be measured, the platform can say how many of the 10 moments each client is winning.

## The 10 moments

| # | Moment | Archetype | Engine | Condition to win | Measurable signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Active hunger | The Hungry Now | Discoverability | 3-pack and delivery app, 4.5★+, photos worth going for | Maps rank, rating, age of the latest photo |
| 2 | Passive scroll | The Passive Discoverer | Acquisition | Repeated exposure with Crave and Vibe reels | Average frequency per person, reels per week |
| 3 | Same category | The Researcher | Discoverability | Rank in the "best [cuisine]" Maps pack, with review volume and recency | Maps rank, total reviews, reviews in 30 days |
| 4 | Category switch | The Passive Discoverer | Acquisition | A Crave reel so good it overrides the original plan | Saves and shares per reel |
| 5 | Something new | The Researcher | Reputation | Review count as legitimacy; distinctive content as personality | Reviews vs local competitors |
| 6 | Friend rec | The Occasion Planner | Reputation | GBP ready, reviews confirm the hype, an easy path to book | GBP completeness, reserve clicks |
| 7 | Occasion | The Occasion Planner | Reputation | Ambience photos, occasion reviews, reservations live | Reservation clicks, occasion mentions |
| 8 | Location-based | The Hungry Now | Discoverability | Maps ranking, consistent NAP, accurate hours, "open now" | NAP consistency, correct "open now" |
| 9 | Delivery app | The Hungry Now | Discoverability | Lead photo quality, platform rating, an active promo | Rating per app, active promo |
| 10 | Viral moment | The Passive Discoverer | Acquisition | Shareable content; operations and GBP ready for the surge | Reach peak vs order peak within 72 h |

Half of the moments (3, 5, 6, 7 and the Maps side of 1) are won with profile, reviews and reservations, not with reach. That is why Discoverability and Reputation are engines of their own.

## The 5 archetypes

| Archetype | Stage | Finds you via | Decides by | The system wins with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Hungry Now | Stranger | Maps, delivery, voice search | Rating, distance and photos, in under 60 s | GBP + 4.5★ + delivery. Operations, not creative |
| The Passive Discoverer | Discovery | A reel in the social feed | Visual impact; recall after 3–10+ views | Crave/Vibe calendar + paid reach |
| The Researcher | Consideration | "best [cuisine] near me", Yelp | Review volume and recency, photos, menu | Review program + GBP + fresh photos |
| The Occasion Planner | First Visit | Editorial lists, "date night" | Atmosphere, sentiment, availability | Vibe content + occasion reviews + booking path |
| The Returning Guest | Loyal Regular | Memory: already on the shortlist | What's new? A reason to return now | CRM email + loyalty habit trigger |

## Eight touchpoints

| Touchpoint | Typical restaurant | With Atrium | The opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Maps search | 3.8★, 40 reviews, photos from 3 years ago | 4.6★+, 200+ reviews, weekly updates, live photos | The 3-pack captures active intent; miss it, lose the guest |
| First social encounter | Inconsistent posts, content as an obligation | Systematic Crave + Vibe calendar built to stop the scroll | Every exposure is a deposit into future recall |
| Pre-visit review research | Low count, unanswered negatives, generic replies | Active generation; negatives handled privately; all answered | A 4.5★ / 200+ profile converts at a far higher rate |
| GBP profile visit | Missing hours, no menu link, stale photos | Complete: photos, menu, posts, hours, buttons live | The digital front door: every answer in under 10 s |
| Delivery app listing | Default photo, competing only on price | Pro lead photo, strong rating, active promo | First photo and rating decide which listing gets clicked |
| Post-first-visit, 24–72 h | Silence: no capture, no follow-up | Welcome flow + return offer + survey + review ask | The highest-intent window for visit #2; most miss it |
| 60-day inactive guest | The guest disappears forever | Win-back flow fires a personalized reactivation offer | Reactivating a lapsed guest costs far less than acquiring one |
| 5th visit milestone | Treated like the first visit | VIP milestone: recognition, benefit, tier upgrade | The 5th visit confirms the habit; recognition compounds it |

## Discovery by generation

Kept as a creative and channel guide, with no metric of its own.

| Generation | Age | Finds via | Trusts | Opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gen Z | 18–27 | TikTok, Instagram Reels | Peer UGC, viral content, aesthetics | One viral Reel can equal months of paid reach |
| Millennial | 28–43 | Instagram, Google | Rating, review volume, food photos | IG + Google + email CRM converts first-timers |
| Gen X | 44–59 | Google, Facebook | Review count, recency, reputation | Facebook + GBP authority + loyalty programs |
| Boomer | 60+ | Google, word of mouth, Facebook | Personal recommendations, high review count | Email + loyalty: the highest-ROI segment |
