## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences on one API, two surfaces (ADR 0016).
- **Platform, the client:** a restaurant owner or location manager, usually not technical, checking in between service. The job: know how guests felt, follow up on a bad visit within 24 hours, hand out the QR, and ask the Atrium team when stuck. Later: see what the Host agent did for them.
- **Manager, the agency:** account managers and admins running a portfolio of clients all day. The job: see what needs a person across clients (overdue cases, approvals), act fast, and keep every client's setup correct.

### Brand Personality
Calm, editorial, precise. The ATRIUM AD design system is the source of truth: cream paper and charcoal ink, one lime signal per view, clay for warmth and warnings, Inter Tight for everything and Instrument Serif for emphasis and figures. No pure white, no pure black.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light work area on cream, charcoal sidebar. Dark mode exists in the tokens but is not the focus.
- **Density differs by audience:** Manager is compact (more rows per screen, tighter rhythm); Platform is airier and simpler.
- Anti-references: generic SaaS admin templates, rows of identical cards, colored side-stripe borders, big hero numbers on every page, gradient text, glass effects.
- A page title is for orientation, not decoration: the top bar already shows the path, so pages do not repeat it as an eyebrow.

### Design Principles
1. **One container, many rows.** A list is one bordered panel with hairline dividers, not a stack of separate cards. Cards are for things with their own actions (a place's QR and settings).
2. **Hierarchy before decoration.** Size, weight and space say what matters; lime appears once per view, clay only when something needs a person.
3. **No side-stripe accents.** Status uses a full border or a tint plus an icon or word, never a thick colored edge.
4. **Say it once.** No eyebrow that repeats the breadcrumb, no intro that restates the heading.
5. **Density follows the job.** Compact for the agency, spacious for the owner, set by one density token, not per-component overrides.
